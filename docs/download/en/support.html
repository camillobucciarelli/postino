Support

Have a problem with Postino, a question or an idea? Write to camillo@bucciarelli.dev: Postino is made by one person, and that person answers.

Reporting a problem

The quickest way is from the app: choose “Report a problem” in Settings. Postino prepares a draft email with the error log; you read it and decide whether to send it. The log may contain subjects and addresses of your messages: how it is handled is in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my messages?

Where they have always been: in your provider's mailbox. Postino keeps an encrypted copy on your device and has no server of its own.

I don't see new mail

Check your connection and that the account is still connected in Settings. If the provider asked you to sign in again, reconnect the account: mail already downloaded stays.

How do I move to a new device?

Install Postino on the new device and connect your accounts again: mail syncs from the providers. On iPhone and iPad the encryption key stays on the device and does not travel with backups: after a restore, connect your accounts again.

How is Postino updated?

From the App Store or the Mac App Store, or from TestFlight if you installed it there. On macOS, if you downloaded the DMG from the website, it updates itself.

More answers, and the macOS and Android downloads, are on the Postino page.
