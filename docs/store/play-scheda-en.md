## Full description (4000)

```
Postino brings all your email accounts into one Inbox that stays fast even offline.

ONE PLACE FOR ALL YOUR MAIL
Connect Gmail, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 (personal and work or school accounts), iCloud and any IMAP mailbox. The unified inbox merges them, sorted by date, and each account has its own color so you see at a glance where a message comes from. You can also open a single account from the sidebar, with its Inbox, Drafts, Sent, Trash and Labels.

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
Recent mail is stored in an encrypted database on your device, so you can read it without a connection. Actions you take offline (archive, star, mark as read) are sent when the network returns; if a message changed elsewhere in the meantime, Postino shows it as a Conflict and you choose which change to keep.

Postino has no mail server of its own: it talks only to the providers you connect. There are no analytics, no ads and no automatic error collection. Remote images are blocked until you choose to show them, and a warning flags links whose text shows a different address from the real one.

SEARCH FAST
Results appear as you type, from the mail on your device. Use from:, to: and subject: to search by sender, recipients and subject, quotes for an exact phrase, and filters for unread, starred, attachments, account and dates. For older mail, the search continues on the provider.

SWIPE ACTIONS
Swipe a row left or right to archive, mark as read, star or delete: you choose which action goes in which direction.

NOTIFICATIONS WITH ACTIONS
New-mail notifications are grouped by account and carry up to two buttons of your choice, for example Archive and Mark as read. By default Postino checks for mail about every 15 minutes. Turn on Immediate notifications and new mail arrives in real time even with Postino closed: in exchange a fixed notification, "Postino is listening", stays on and battery use goes up.

LABELS, EVEN AUTOMATIC ONES
Create labels, even nested ones ("Projects/Postino"), and apply them to messages. With a Gmail account you can turn on automatic labeling for a label, with a description: Postino assigns it by itself to new messages that resemble it. The model runs on your device and the content of your messages is never sent to any service. The model downloads once, the first time you turn it on (about 165 MB), over Wi-Fi or after your confirmation on a cellular network.

WRITE AT YOUR OWN PACE
Reply, reply all and forward, with the original message quoted. Drafts are saved on the provider, so you find them in another client too. After you tap Send you have a moment to undo; if you are offline, the message waits in a queue and goes out by itself when the network returns.

SETTINGS ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
If you turn it on, Postino keeps your connected accounts, colors, swipe actions and automatic labels in sync across your devices, in an encrypted file in the space reserved for the app in your own Google Drive. The key stays on your devices: a new device is paired by scanning a QR code or typing a code. Never your mail.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
"Report a problem" prepares an email with the error log: you read it and decide whether to send it. Postino never sends anything by itself.

Postino is available in Italian and English.
```
